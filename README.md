# Larpathon
### Larp your way up to the top!

---

**Larpathon** is a free-to-use roleplay platform built for people who want to create, explore, and experience their own roleplay journeys without paying for access.

Inspired by platforms like [LarpLabs](https://larplabs.io/), Larpathon aims to make roleplay more accessible to everyone. Whether you're starting your journey, building your character, or working your way toward the top, Larpathon is about giving you the freedom to play your way.

## ✨ What is Larpathon?

Larpathon is designed around one simple idea: **roleplay should be accessible to everyone.**

No paywalls. No required subscriptions. Just a platform where users can enjoy the experience without worrying about the cost of entry.

## 🚀 Our Goals

- **Completely Free** — Access the platform without mandatory subscriptions or paid access.
- **Built for Roleplay** — Create your own journey and immerse yourself in roleplay experiences.
- **Progress Your Way** — Work your way up, develop your experience, and reach new heights.
- **Community First** — Make roleplay more welcoming and accessible to everyone.
- **Always Improving** — Continue evolving the platform based on community needs and feedback.

## 🎯 Our Mission

Our mission is to make roleplay accessible to everyone by building a platform where creativity, progression, and community matter more than money.

We believe everyone should have the opportunity to experience roleplay without being held back by a paywall.
